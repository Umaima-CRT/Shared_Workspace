# **SECTION 9: BEFORE YOU ASK (FAQs)** 

# **Questions We Hear a Lot.** 

# **How is this different from support?** 

Support reacts to issues. Account management proactively owns the relationship, driving renewals, spotting expansion opportunities and keeping customers loyal. The two work well together, but account management is about protecting and growing revenue, not just resolving tickets. 

# **Will managers work in our CRM?** 

Yes. Everything, check-ins, renewal tracking, notes and opportunities, lives inside your CRM, so you have full visibility and the relationship history stays with your business. 

# **Will customers know it is outsourced?** 

We work as an extension of your team, in your brand and tone, so the experience is seamless. Customers get a consistent, knowledgeable point of contact, which is what actually drives loyalty. 

# **How do you find upsell opportunities?** 

By actually knowing the accounts. Regular check-ins and health monitoring surface needs and growth signals, and we introduce the right offer at the right moment, helpfully, not pushily. 

# **Can you handle just our key accounts?** 

Yes. Many clients start with their highest-value accounts to prove the retention and expansion impact, then expand coverage. We prioritize by account value and renewal timing. 

# **Can you manage both B2B and B2C customer accounts?** 

Yes. Our account management approach can support B2B relationships and suitable B2C account models, depending on your customer base and business requirements. We align check-ins, communication, retention activities, and growth opportunities with the type of relationships you need to manage. 

# **How do you measure account management performance?** 

We track relevant performance indicators such as customer retention, renewal rates, churn, account engagement, and upsell or cross-sell opportunities. Reporting can be aligned with your CRM and business objectives so you can understand account health and identify areas for improvement. 

# **Can you manage contract renewals and follow-ups?** 

Yes. Our account managers can monitor renewal dates, schedule proactive check-ins, follow up with customers, and coordinate renewal discussions according to your processes. This helps reduce missed deadlines and gives your team more time to address potential concerns before renewal. 

# **How do you handle unhappy or at-risk customers?** 

We use regular communication and account health monitoring to identify signs of dissatisfaction or declining engagement. Our managers can follow up, understand customer concerns, coordinate solutions with your team, and escalate issues when needed to help protect the relationship. 

# **How quickly can we get started with account management services?** 

The timeline depends on the number of accounts, CRM setup, renewal schedules, and the complexity of your customer relationships. We begin by understanding your goals and existing processes, then establish onboarding, account priorities, and a management plan before service begins.
