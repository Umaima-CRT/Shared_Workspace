# **<mark>SECTION 10: BEFORE YOU ASK (FAQs)</mark>** 

# **Questions We Hear a Lot.** 

# **Will agents know our product well enough?** 

Yes. Thorough onboarding is core to how we work. We train agents on your product, systems and tone, build playbooks and a knowledge base, and QA their work so customers get accurate, on-brand help. Agents ramp before they ever touch a live customer. 

# **Do you work inside our existing helpdesk?** 

Yes. Our agents work inside your existing helpdesk, CRM and knowledge base, so everything stays in your systems and your data. No migration required and a seamless experience for your customers. 

# **Can you cover nights and weekends?** 

Yes. We can extend coverage to nights, weekends and holidays so issues do not sit unanswered, and we can flex the team up for busy seasons without you over-hiring permanent staff. 

# **How do you keep quality high?** 

We hold agents to your SLAs, run continuous QA and coaching, and report on response times, resolution rates and CSAT. You get transparency into exactly how support is performing. 

# **Can we start small?** 

Yes. Many clients start with a small team to handle overflow or a specific channel, then scale as they see the quality and impact. We flex the team around your volume. 

# **Can your agents handle technical troubleshooting?** 

Yes. Our agents can provide Tier-1 and Tier-2 technical support based on your product, documentation, and escalation procedures. More complex issues can be routed to your internal technical team with the relevant details so they can investigate efficiently. 

# **Which customer support channels can you handle?** 

Depending on your requirements, our team can support inbound channels such as phone, email, live chat, and helpdesk tickets. We'll determine the appropriate channel coverage based on your customers' needs, support volume, and existing systems. 

# **How quickly can you onboard an inbound support team?** 

The onboarding timeline depends on your support volume, product complexity, tools, and training requirements. We use the onboarding period to understand your workflows, prepare support documentation, train agents, and establish quality standards before going live. 

# **Can your support team follow our existing escalation procedures?** 

Yes. We can work with your existing escalation rules, response priorities, and internal workflows. Agents follow agreed procedures for resolving common issues, identifying urgent cases, and transferring complex problems to the appropriate team. 

# **How do you handle customer data and confidential information?** 

Our support workflows can be aligned with your data-handling policies, access controls, and security requirements. During onboarding, we establish the appropriate procedures for accessing customer information, using support systems, and handling confidential data.
